About

I am a multidisciplinary artist based in Førde, Norway. My work explores the relationship between humans and nature, often through a lens of sustainability and material awareness. I am drawn to art as an open Space, where emotions are allowed to emerge, rather than to be controlled. I aim to create work that meet the Viewer where they are.

Much of my practice involves working with available or repurposed materials, inviting unpredictability and experimentation. My work includes drawing, painting, photography, sculpture and redesign. I would love to talk if you have any questions or would like to collaborate.
